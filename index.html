About Us

Why SageFellow exists

We're a small startup, not a research lab with a marketing budget — and that shapes everything we build.

SageFellow AI started because we kept running into the same problem: AI coding tools that look great in a demo video and fall apart the moment you point them at a real, messy codebase.

So we're building the tools we actually wanted for ourselves — a coding chatbot that understands context, and a website builder that hands you real code instead of trapping you inside someone else's platform.

We're still small. That means slower growth, but it also means every feature gets used by the people who built it before it ever reaches you.
